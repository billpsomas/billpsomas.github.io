[CV4GOOD](https://cv4good.thegoodailab.org/), the first edition of the `Computer Vision for Humanitarian Action` workshop, was accepted at ECCV 2026. I am a member of the organizing committee.

The workshop brings together computer vision researchers and frontline humanitarian practitioners around real-world problems in earth observation and medical imaging. It is co-hosted by [The Good AI Lab](https://thegoodailab.org/) and Médecins Sans Frontières, and takes place on September 8, 2026 in Malmö, Sweden.
