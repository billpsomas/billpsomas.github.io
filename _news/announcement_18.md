[CV4GOOD](https://cv4good.thegoodailab.org/), the first `Computer Vision for Humanitarian Action` workshop at ECCV 2026, takes place today at Malmömässan in Malmö, Sweden. I am one of its organizers.

The workshop pairs researchers building vision and Earth observation pipelines with humanitarian practitioners who can say what works in the field, what fails, and what is missing. It is co-hosted by [The Good AI Lab](https://thegoodailab.org/) and Médecins Sans Frontières.

The half-day program opens with needs from the field, moves through deployed Earth observation and conflict monitoring systems, and closes with a panel on dual use and governance. Speakers come from MSF, GEOHUM at the University of Salzburg, the Conflict Ecology Lab at Oregon State, MIT, and the Allen Institute for AI.
