I was an organizer of [CV4GOOD](https://cv4good.thegoodailab.org/), the first `Computer Vision for Humanitarian Action` workshop at ECCV 2026, held on September 8 at Malmömässan in Malmö, Sweden.

The workshop pairs researchers building vision and Earth observation pipelines with humanitarian practitioners who can say what works in the field, what fails, and what is missing. It is co-hosted by [The Good AI Lab](https://thegoodailab.org/) and Médecins Sans Frontières.

The half-day program opened with needs from the field, moved through deployed Earth observation and conflict monitoring systems, and closed with a panel on dual use and governance. Speakers came from MSF, GEOHUM at the University of Salzburg, the Conflict Ecology Lab at Oregon State, MIT, and the Allen Institute for AI.
