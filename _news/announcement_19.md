I became a member of the [ELLIS Society](https://ellis.eu/members), the European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems.

ELLIS is a pan-European network of scientists working in machine learning and AI, with membership requiring a doctorate, a record of publication at top-tier venues, and a clear connection to Europe.
