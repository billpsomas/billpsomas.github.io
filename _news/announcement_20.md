I was invited to serve as an `Evaluator` for the [ELLIS PhD Program](https://ellis.eu/research/phd-postdoc) in the 2026–2027 recruiting round.

The program places doctoral students with two supervisors at different European institutions. Evaluators review applications during pre-screening, which runs from November 9 to 23, 2026.
