I am a postdoctoral researcher in Computer Vision and Machine Learning working at the Visual Recognition Group of Czech Technical University in Prague. I was recently awarded the MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship 2024 for my project Retrieval-Augmented VIsion-Language Models for Open-vocabulary LocalizatIon (RAVIOLI).

I received my Ph.D. in September 2024 from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA), where I defended my dissertation, Learning Visual and Multimodal Representations, under the supervision of Konstantinos Karantzalos and Yannis Avrithis.

I have gained research experience through internships at leading institutes such as Inria Rennes, Institute of Advanced Research in Artificial Intelligence (IARAI), and Athena Research Center. I have published 7 papers in A* venues including CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICLR, and NeurIPS, in addition to journal and workshop publications. Beyond research, I contribute to the academic community through reviewing, teaching, workshop organization, and student supervision.
