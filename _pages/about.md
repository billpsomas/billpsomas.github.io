I am an MSCA Postdoctoral Fellow in Computer Vision and Machine Learning at the [Visual Recognition Group](https://vrg.fel.cvut.cz/) of [Czech Technical University in Prague (CTU)](https://www.cvut.cz/en). My project, RAVIOLI (Retrieval-Augmented VIsion-Language Models for Open-vocabulary LocalizatIon), is funded through the [MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2024](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/calls/msca-postdoctoral-fellowships-2024). I am also affiliated with the [Good AI Lab](https://thegoodailab.org/) and am a member of the [ELLIS Society](https://ellis.eu/members), where I serve as an evaluator for the ELLIS PhD program.

I received my Ph.D. from the [National Technical University of Athens (NTUA)](https://ntua.gr/en/) in September 2024. My dissertation, [Learning Visual and Multimodal Representations](https://freader.ekt.gr/eadd/index.php?doc=57401&lang=en), was supervised by [Konstantinos Karantzalos](http://users.ntua.gr/karank/) and [Yannis Avrithis](https://avrithis.net/).

Previously, I held research positions and internships at [Inria Rennes](https://www.inria.fr/en/inria-centre-rennes-university), the [Institute of Advanced Research in Artificial Intelligence (IARAI)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/iarai/), and [Athena Research Center](https://www.athenarc.gr/en). I have published eight papers at A* conferences, including CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICLR, and NeurIPS, alongside journal and workshop publications. I serve as an Area Chair for [ICLR 2027](https://iclr.cc/) and [ICML 2027](https://icml.cc/). My academic service also includes reviewing, teaching, workshop organization, and student supervision.

I teach graduate courses in Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Earth Observation:

- `Advanced Methods of Remote Sensing` — NTUA, MSc in Geoinformatics, spring semester, since 2022.
- `Computer Vision` — NKUA, MSc in Data Science and Information Technologies, winter semester, since 2024.
- `Advanced Deep Learning` — CTU, MSc in Open Informatics - Artificial Intelligence, spring semester, since 2026.
